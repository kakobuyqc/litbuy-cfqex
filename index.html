03 / Litbuy spreadsheet guide

Browse. Compare. Keep notes.

A short, repeatable process makes your Litbuy finds easier to revisit and compare.

01

Choose one category

Start with shoes, a clothing layer or an accessory. Save a direct listing URL and the specific variant you are researching, rather than an image alone.

02

Separate facts from guesses

Copy measurements and stated materials into separate columns. Mark missing information as unknown. A product photo alone cannot confirm composition or authenticity.

03

Review the whole cost

Keep item price, currency, delivery and applicable fees separate. Add a checked-on date so old prices and unavailable listings are easy to spot.

05 / Fit before the finish

Make the details work for you.

Clothing: measure a familiar piece

For hoodies, sweaters, jackets and T-shirts, compare a listing's measurements with a similar garment you already wear. Check whether a measurement is taken flat or around the body. Keep centimeters and inches clearly labeled.

Footwear: record the chart source

Do not assume that a size label transfers between models. Save the listing's size chart and distinguish foot length from insole length when either is provided.

Accessories: scale matters

A Litbuy accessories spreadsheet should include dimensions. For headwear and bracelets, note circumference or the stated fit range. For jewelry, keep any material claim tied to its source.

Before you shortlist

A useful research checklist

  • Can you identify the exact item and selected variant?
  • Are measurements available, with clear units?
  • Are the images listing photos, reference photos or customer photos?
  • Have you recorded missing details instead of assuming them?
  • Are estimated charges separated from confirmed prices?
  • Have you checked the seller's terms and any applicable destination restrictions?

06 / Clear answers

Litbuy spreadsheet FAQ

Practical answers about research sheets, sizing and image references.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a shopping research sheet used to organize product links and notes. Useful fields include category, listing URL, measurements, price, photo references and the date each detail was checked.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page provides a category directory and a practical comparison guide. Its browsing links take you to CAFANS.

How should I compare clothing sizes?

Compare the listing's garment measurements with a similar item you own. Check units, measurement method and the selected size rather than relying only on S, M or L labels.

Do reference images confirm CAFANS availability or authenticity?

No. The brand images are visual references. They do not establish stock, seller identity, authenticity, authorization or a relationship between the brands and CAFANS.

What costs belong in a shopping spreadsheet?

Record the item price and currency, domestic delivery, applicable service fees, international shipping estimates, and any destination taxes or duties. Mark estimates separately from confirmed charges.
